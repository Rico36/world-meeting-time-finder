Frequently asked questions

Meeting time-zone questions

Does Common Hours account for daylight saving time?

Yes. Times are calculated for the selected meeting date using each city’s time zone, including applicable daylight-saving changes.

Are public holidays excluded from the results?

No. Holidays are displayed as notices so you can make the final decision rather than having possible meeting times removed automatically.

What does the green area mean?

Green represents normal weekday working hours for each city. The darker band shows the meeting time you are currently considering.

Do I need an account?

No. Common Hours is free to use and does not require registration. Your selected cities and preferences stay in your browser.
