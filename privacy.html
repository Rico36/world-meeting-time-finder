Privacy

A simple tool with minimal data collection

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Information stored on your device

Common Hours stores selected cities, language and appearance preferences in your browser so they can be restored on a later visit. This information is not currently sent to a Common Hours account or database.

External data services

When you search for a city or check a public holiday, your browser contacts third-party data services to retrieve matching locations, time zones and holiday information. Those providers may receive normal request information such as your IP address and browser details.

Analytics

Common Hours uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits, page views, referring websites, countries, device types and page performance. Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-first analytics that does not collect or use visitors’ personal data. Common Hours does not use this information to identify individual visitors.

Advertising

Common Hours uses Google AdSense to display and measure advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and similar technologies to serve, personalize and measure ads, subject to your consent where required. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services in Google's privacy information and manage advertising preferences through My Ad Center.

Your choices

You can clear saved Common Hours preferences at any time by clearing this site’s browser storage. No account is required to use the tool.
