Questions about Mexico City and Tokyo

What time is it in Tokyo when it is 9 AM in Mexico City?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Mexico City is 12 AM in Tokyo (UTC-6 against UTC+9). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Mexico City or Tokyo change clocks first?

Neither Mexico City nor Tokyo observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Mexico City or Tokyo soon?

Mexico City: Independence Day on 16 September. Tokyo: Respect for the Aged Day on 21 September.
