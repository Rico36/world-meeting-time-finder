The time difference

For most of the year, Mumbai is 4.5 hours ahead of London. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Mumbai is 5.5 hours ahead of London
  • 29 March to 24 October: Mumbai is 4.5 hours ahead of London
  • 25 October to 31 December: Mumbai is 5.5 hours ahead of London

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in London lands at 1:30 PM in Mumbai — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about London and Mumbai

What time is it in Mumbai when it is 9 AM in London?

For most of the year, 9 AM in London is 1:30 PM in Mumbai (UTC+1 against UTC+5:30). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does London or Mumbai change clocks first?

Mumbai does not observe daylight saving time; London does, so the gap shifts when London changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in London or Mumbai soon?

London: Christmas Day on 25 December. Mumbai: Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on 2 October.
