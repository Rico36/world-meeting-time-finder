The time difference

For most of the year, Mumbai is 10.5 hours ahead of Chicago. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Mumbai is 11.5 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 8 March to 31 October: Mumbai is 10.5 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 1 November to 31 December: Mumbai is 11.5 hours ahead of Chicago

Best time to meet

Chicago and Mumbai do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 7 AM in Chicago / 5:30 PM in Mumbai — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Clock changes to plan around

  • Chicago changes clocks — 2026: 8 March and 1 November; 2027: 14 March and 7 November.
  • Mumbai does not change clocks.

Questions about Chicago and Mumbai

What time is it in Mumbai when it is 9 AM in Chicago?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Chicago is 7:30 PM in Mumbai (UTC-5 against UTC+5:30). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Chicago or Mumbai change clocks first?

Mumbai does not observe daylight saving time; Chicago does, so the gap shifts when Chicago changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Chicago or Mumbai soon?

Chicago: Columbus Day on 12 October. Mumbai: Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on 2 October.
