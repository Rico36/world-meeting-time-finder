The time difference

For most of the year, Berlin is 6 hours ahead of New York. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Berlin is 6 hours ahead of New York
  • 8 March to 28 March: Berlin is 5 hours ahead of New York
  • 29 March to 24 October: Berlin is 6 hours ahead of New York
  • 25 October to 31 October: Berlin is 5 hours ahead of New York
  • 1 November to 31 December: Berlin is 6 hours ahead of New York

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 3 PM in Berlin lands at 9 AM in New York — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Berlin and New York

When is the best time to meet between Berlin and New York?

Aim for 3 PM in Berlin, which is 9 AM in New York. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in New York when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 3 AM in New York (UTC+2 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or New York change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or New York soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. New York: Columbus Day on 12 October.
