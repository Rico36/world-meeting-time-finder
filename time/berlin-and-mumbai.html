The time difference

For most of the year, Mumbai is 3.5 hours ahead of Berlin. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Mumbai is 4.5 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 29 March to 24 October: Mumbai is 3.5 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 25 October to 31 December: Mumbai is 4.5 hours ahead of Berlin

Questions about Berlin and Mumbai

What time is it in Mumbai when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 12:30 PM in Mumbai (UTC+2 against UTC+5:30). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or Mumbai change clocks first?

Mumbai does not observe daylight saving time; Berlin does, so the gap shifts when Berlin changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or Mumbai soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. Mumbai: Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on 2 October.
