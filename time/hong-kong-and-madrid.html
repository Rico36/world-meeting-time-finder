The time difference

For most of the year, Hong Kong is 6 hours ahead of Madrid. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Hong Kong is 7 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 29 March to 24 October: Hong Kong is 6 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 25 October to 31 December: Hong Kong is 7 hours ahead of Madrid

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 3 PM in Hong Kong lands at 9 AM in Madrid — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Hong Kong and Madrid

What time is it in Madrid when it is 9 AM in Hong Kong?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Hong Kong is 3 AM in Madrid (UTC+8 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Hong Kong or Madrid change clocks first?

Hong Kong does not observe daylight saving time; Madrid does, so the gap shifts when Madrid changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Hong Kong or Madrid soon?

Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September. Madrid: National Day on 12 October.
