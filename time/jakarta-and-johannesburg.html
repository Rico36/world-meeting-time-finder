Questions about Jakarta and Johannesburg

For most of the year, 9 AM in Jakarta is 4 AM in Johannesburg (UTC+7 against UTC+2). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Jakarta or Johannesburg change clocks first?

Neither Jakarta nor Johannesburg observes daylight saving time.
