The time difference

For most of the year, Mumbai is 9.5 hours ahead of Toronto. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Mumbai is 10.5 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 8 March to 31 October: Mumbai is 9.5 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 1 November to 31 December: Mumbai is 10.5 hours ahead of Toronto

Best time to meet

Mumbai and Toronto do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 5 PM in Mumbai / 7:30 AM in Toronto — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Mumbai and Toronto

When is the best time to meet between Mumbai and Toronto?

There is no hour that is inside normal working time in both cities, so someone has to stretch. The least painful slot is 5 PM in Mumbai / 7:30 AM in Toronto. At this distance the fair answer is usually to alternate which side takes the awkward hour rather than fixing one time forever.

What time is it in Toronto when it is 9 AM in Mumbai?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Mumbai is 11:30 PM in Toronto (UTC+5:30 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Mumbai or Toronto change clocks first?

Mumbai does not observe daylight saving time; Toronto does, so the gap shifts when Toronto changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Mumbai or Toronto soon?

Mumbai: Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on 2 October. Toronto: Christmas Day on 25 December.
