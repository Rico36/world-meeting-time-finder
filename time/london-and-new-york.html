The time difference

For most of the year, London is 5 hours ahead of New York. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: London is 5 hours ahead of New York
  • 8 March to 28 March: London is 4 hours ahead of New York
  • 29 March to 24 October: London is 5 hours ahead of New York
  • 25 October to 31 October: London is 4 hours ahead of New York
  • 1 November to 31 December: London is 5 hours ahead of New York

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 2 PM in London lands at 9 AM in New York — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about London and New York

What time is it in New York when it is 9 AM in London?

For most of the year, 9 AM in London is 4 AM in New York (UTC+1 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does London or New York change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in London or New York soon?

London: Christmas Day on 25 December. New York: Columbus Day on 12 October.
