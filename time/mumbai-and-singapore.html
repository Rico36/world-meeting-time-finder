Questions about Mumbai and Singapore

What time is it in Singapore when it is 9 AM in Mumbai?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Mumbai is 11:30 AM in Singapore (UTC+5:30 against UTC+8). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Mumbai or Singapore change clocks first?

Neither Mumbai nor Singapore observes daylight saving time.
