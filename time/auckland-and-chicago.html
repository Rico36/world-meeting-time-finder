The time difference

For most of the year, Auckland is 17 hours ahead of Chicago. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Auckland is 19 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 8 March to 4 April: Auckland is 18 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 5 April to 26 September: Auckland is 17 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 27 September to 31 October: Auckland is 18 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 1 November to 31 December: Auckland is 19 hours ahead of Chicago

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in Auckland lands at 4 PM in Chicago — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Auckland and Chicago

When is the best time to meet between Auckland and Chicago?

Aim for 9 AM in Auckland, which is 4 PM in Chicago. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Chicago when it is 9 AM in Auckland?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Auckland is 4 PM in Chicago (UTC+12 against UTC-5). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Auckland or Chicago change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Auckland or Chicago soon?

Auckland: Labour Day on 26 October. Chicago: Columbus Day on 12 October.
