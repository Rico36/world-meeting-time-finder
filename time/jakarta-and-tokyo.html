Questions about Jakarta and Tokyo

What time is it in Tokyo when it is 9 AM in Jakarta?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Jakarta is 11 AM in Tokyo (UTC+7 against UTC+9). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Jakarta or Tokyo change clocks first?

Neither Jakarta nor Tokyo observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Jakarta or Tokyo soon?

Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December. Tokyo: Respect for the Aged Day on 21 September.
