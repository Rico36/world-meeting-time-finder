The time difference

For most of the year, Dubai is 2 hours ahead of Berlin. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Dubai is 3 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 29 March to 24 October: Dubai is 2 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 25 October to 31 December: Dubai is 3 hours ahead of Berlin

Questions about Berlin and Dubai

What time is it in Dubai when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 11 AM in Dubai (UTC+2 against UTC+4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or Dubai change clocks first?

Dubai does not observe daylight saving time; Berlin does, so the gap shifts when Berlin changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or Dubai soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. Dubai: National Day on 2 December.
