The time difference

The gap between Amsterdam and London is constant all year: Amsterdam is 1 hours ahead of London.

Questions about Amsterdam and London

What time is it in London when it is 9 AM in Amsterdam?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Amsterdam is 8 AM in London (UTC+2 against UTC+1). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Amsterdam or London change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Amsterdam or London soon?

Amsterdam: Christmas Day on 25 December. London: Christmas Day on 25 December.
