Questions about Dubai and Hong Kong

What time is it in Hong Kong when it is 9 AM in Dubai?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Dubai is 1 PM in Hong Kong (UTC+4 against UTC+8). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Dubai or Hong Kong change clocks first?

Neither Dubai nor Hong Kong observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Dubai or Hong Kong soon?

Dubai: National Day on 2 December. Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September.
