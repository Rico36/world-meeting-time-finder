Questions about Hong Kong and Singapore

What time is it in Singapore when it is 9 AM in Hong Kong?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Hong Kong is 9 AM in Singapore (UTC+8 against UTC+8). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Hong Kong or Singapore change clocks first?

Neither Hong Kong nor Singapore observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Hong Kong or Singapore soon?

Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September. Singapore: Diwali on 8 November.
