The time difference

For most of the year, Sydney is 8 hours ahead of Berlin. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Sydney is 10 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 29 March to 4 April: Sydney is 9 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 5 April to 3 October: Sydney is 8 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 4 October to 24 October: Sydney is 9 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 25 October to 31 December: Sydney is 10 hours ahead of Berlin

Best time to meet

Berlin and Sydney do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 8:30 AM in Berlin / 4:30 PM in Sydney — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Berlin and Sydney

When is the best time to meet between Berlin and Sydney?

There is no hour that is inside normal working time in both cities, so someone has to stretch. The least painful slot is 8:30 AM in Berlin / 4:30 PM in Sydney. At this distance the fair answer is usually to alternate which side takes the awkward hour rather than fixing one time forever.

What time is it in Sydney when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 5 PM in Sydney (UTC+2 against UTC+10). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or Sydney change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or Sydney soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. Sydney: Christmas Day on 25 December.
