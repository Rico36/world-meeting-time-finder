The time difference

For most of the year, Auckland is 6.5 hours ahead of Mumbai. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 4 April: Auckland is 7.5 hours ahead of Mumbai
  • 5 April to 26 September: Auckland is 6.5 hours ahead of Mumbai
  • 27 September to 31 December: Auckland is 7.5 hours ahead of Mumbai

Questions about Auckland and Mumbai

What time is it in Mumbai when it is 9 AM in Auckland?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Auckland is 2:30 AM in Mumbai (UTC+12 against UTC+5:30). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Auckland or Mumbai change clocks first?

Mumbai does not observe daylight saving time; Auckland does, so the gap shifts when Auckland changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Auckland or Mumbai soon?

Auckland: Labour Day on 26 October. Mumbai: Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on 2 October.
