The time difference

For most of the year, Tokyo is 7 hours ahead of Berlin. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Tokyo is 8 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 29 March to 24 October: Tokyo is 7 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 25 October to 31 December: Tokyo is 8 hours ahead of Berlin

Questions about Berlin and Tokyo

When is the best time to meet between Berlin and Tokyo?

Aim for 9 AM in Berlin, which is 4 PM in Tokyo. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Tokyo when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 4 PM in Tokyo (UTC+2 against UTC+9). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or Tokyo change clocks first?

Tokyo does not observe daylight saving time; Berlin does, so the gap shifts when Berlin changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or Tokyo soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. Tokyo: Respect for the Aged Day on 21 September.
