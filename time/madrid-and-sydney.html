The time difference

For most of the year, Sydney is 8 hours ahead of Madrid. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Sydney is 10 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 29 March to 4 April: Sydney is 9 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 5 April to 3 October: Sydney is 8 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 4 October to 24 October: Sydney is 9 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 25 October to 31 December: Sydney is 10 hours ahead of Madrid

Best time to meet

Madrid and Sydney do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 8:30 AM in Madrid / 4:30 PM in Sydney — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Madrid and Sydney

What time is it in Sydney when it is 9 AM in Madrid?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Madrid is 5 PM in Sydney (UTC+2 against UTC+10). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Madrid or Sydney change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Madrid or Sydney soon?

Madrid: National Day on 12 October. Sydney: Christmas Day on 25 December.
