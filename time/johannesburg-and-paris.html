The time difference

For most of the year, the two cities share the same local time. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Johannesburg is 1 hours ahead of Paris
  • 29 March to 24 October: same local time
  • 25 October to 31 December: Johannesburg is 1 hours ahead of Paris

Questions about Johannesburg and Paris

What time is it in Paris when it is 9 AM in Johannesburg?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Johannesburg is 9 AM in Paris (UTC+2 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Johannesburg or Paris change clocks first?

Johannesburg does not observe daylight saving time; Paris does, so the gap shifts when Paris changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Johannesburg or Paris soon?

Johannesburg: Heritage Day on 24 September. Paris: All Saints' Day on 1 November.
