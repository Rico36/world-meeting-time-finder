The time difference

For most of the year, the two cities share the same local time. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Johannesburg is 1 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 29 March to 24 October: same local time

Questions about Johannesburg and Madrid

What time is it in Madrid when it is 9 AM in Johannesburg?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Johannesburg is 9 AM in Madrid (UTC+2 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Johannesburg or Madrid change clocks first?

Johannesburg does not observe daylight saving time; Madrid does, so the gap shifts when Madrid changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Johannesburg or Madrid soon?

Johannesburg: Heritage Day on 24 September. Madrid: National Day on 12 October.
