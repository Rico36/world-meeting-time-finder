Questions about Chicago and Johannesburg

When is the best time to meet between Chicago and Johannesburg?

Aim for 9 AM in Chicago, which is 4 PM in Johannesburg. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Johannesburg when it is 9 AM in Chicago?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Chicago is 4 PM in Johannesburg (UTC-5 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Chicago or Johannesburg change clocks first?

Johannesburg does not observe daylight saving time; Chicago does, so the gap shifts when Chicago changes clocks.
