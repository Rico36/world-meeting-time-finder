The time difference

For most of the year, the two cities share the same local time. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Johannesburg is 1 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 29 March to 24 October: same local time
  • 25 October to 31 December: Johannesburg is 1 hours ahead of Berlin

Questions about Berlin and Johannesburg

What time is it in Johannesburg when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 9 AM in Johannesburg (UTC+2 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or Johannesburg change clocks first?

Johannesburg does not observe daylight saving time; Berlin does, so the gap shifts when Berlin changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or Johannesburg soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. Johannesburg: Heritage Day on 24 September.
