The time difference

For most of the year, Chicago is 1 hours ahead of Mexico City. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: same local time
  • 8 March to 31 October: Chicago is 1 hours ahead of Mexico City
  • 1 November to 31 December: same local time

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 10 AM in Chicago lands at 9 AM in Mexico City — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Chicago and Mexico City

When is the best time to meet between Chicago and Mexico City?

Aim for 10 AM in Chicago, which is 9 AM in Mexico City. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Mexico City when it is 9 AM in Chicago?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Chicago is 8 AM in Mexico City (UTC-5 against UTC-6). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Chicago or Mexico City change clocks first?

Mexico City does not observe daylight saving time; Chicago does, so the gap shifts when Chicago changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Chicago or Mexico City soon?

Chicago: Columbus Day on 12 October. Mexico City: Independence Day on 16 September.
