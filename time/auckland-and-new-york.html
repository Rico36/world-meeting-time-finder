The time difference

For most of the year, Auckland is 16 hours ahead of New York. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Auckland is 18 hours ahead of New York
  • 8 March to 4 April: Auckland is 17 hours ahead of New York
  • 5 April to 26 September: Auckland is 16 hours ahead of New York
  • 27 September to 31 October: Auckland is 17 hours ahead of New York
  • 1 November to 31 December: Auckland is 18 hours ahead of New York

Best time to meet

Auckland and New York do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 8:30 AM in Auckland / 4:30 PM in New York — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Auckland and New York

What time is it in New York when it is 9 AM in Auckland?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Auckland is 5 PM in New York (UTC+12 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Auckland or New York change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Auckland or New York soon?

Auckland: Labour Day on 26 October. New York: Columbus Day on 12 October.
