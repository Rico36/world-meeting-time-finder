The time difference

For most of the year, Seoul is 13 hours ahead of Toronto. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Seoul is 14 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 8 March to 31 October: Seoul is 13 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 1 November to 31 December: Seoul is 14 hours ahead of Toronto

Best time to meet

Seoul and Toronto do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 7 AM in Seoul / 6 PM in Toronto — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Clock changes to plan around

  • Seoul does not change clocks.
  • Toronto changes clocks — 2026: 8 March and 1 November; 2027: 14 March and 7 November.

Questions about Seoul and Toronto

What time is it in Toronto when it is 9 AM in Seoul?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Seoul is 8 PM in Toronto (UTC+9 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Seoul or Toronto change clocks first?

Seoul does not observe daylight saving time; Toronto does, so the gap shifts when Toronto changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Seoul or Toronto soon?

Seoul: The day preceding Chuseok on 24 September. Toronto: Christmas Day on 25 December.
