The time difference

For most of the year, Mexico City is 1 hours ahead of Los Angeles. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Mexico City is 2 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 8 March to 31 October: Mexico City is 1 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 1 November to 31 December: Mexico City is 2 hours ahead of Los Angeles

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in Los Angeles lands at 10 AM in Mexico City — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Los Angeles and Mexico City

What time is it in Mexico City when it is 9 AM in Los Angeles?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Los Angeles is 10 AM in Mexico City (UTC-7 against UTC-6). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Los Angeles or Mexico City change clocks first?

Mexico City does not observe daylight saving time; Los Angeles does, so the gap shifts when Los Angeles changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Los Angeles or Mexico City soon?

Los Angeles: Columbus Day on 12 October. Mexico City: Independence Day on 16 September.
