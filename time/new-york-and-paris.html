The time difference

For most of the year, Paris is 6 hours ahead of New York. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Paris is 6 hours ahead of New York
  • 8 March to 28 March: Paris is 5 hours ahead of New York
  • 29 March to 24 October: Paris is 6 hours ahead of New York
  • 25 October to 31 October: Paris is 5 hours ahead of New York
  • 1 November to 31 December: Paris is 6 hours ahead of New York

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in New York lands at 3 PM in Paris — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about New York and Paris

What time is it in Paris when it is 9 AM in New York?

For most of the year, 9 AM in New York is 3 PM in Paris (UTC-4 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does New York or Paris change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in New York or Paris soon?

New York: Columbus Day on 12 October. Paris: All Saints' Day on 1 November.
