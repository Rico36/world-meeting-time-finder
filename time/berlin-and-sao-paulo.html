The time difference

For most of the year, Berlin is 5 hours ahead of São Paulo. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Berlin is 4 hours ahead of São Paulo
  • 29 March to 24 October: Berlin is 5 hours ahead of São Paulo
  • 25 October to 31 December: Berlin is 4 hours ahead of São Paulo

Questions about Berlin and São Paulo

What time is it in São Paulo when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 4 AM in São Paulo (UTC+2 against UTC-3). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or São Paulo change clocks first?

São Paulo does not observe daylight saving time; Berlin does, so the gap shifts when Berlin changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or São Paulo soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. São Paulo: Our Lady of Aparecida on 12 October.
