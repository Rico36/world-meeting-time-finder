The Sydney time zone

Sydney moves between UTC+10 and UTC+11 across the year. Sydney changes clocks — 2026: 5 April and 4 October; 2027: 4 April and 3 October. Any city pairing below that involves a fixed-offset city will shift by an hour on those dates.
