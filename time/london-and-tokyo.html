The time difference

For most of the year, Tokyo is 8 hours ahead of London. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Tokyo is 9 hours ahead of London
  • 29 March to 24 October: Tokyo is 8 hours ahead of London
  • 25 October to 31 December: Tokyo is 9 hours ahead of London

Best time to meet

London and Tokyo do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 8:30 AM in London / 4:30 PM in Tokyo — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about London and Tokyo

What time is it in Tokyo when it is 9 AM in London?

For most of the year, 9 AM in London is 5 PM in Tokyo (UTC+1 against UTC+9). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does London or Tokyo change clocks first?

Tokyo does not observe daylight saving time; London does, so the gap shifts when London changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in London or Tokyo soon?

London: Christmas Day on 25 December. Tokyo: Respect for the Aged Day on 21 September.
