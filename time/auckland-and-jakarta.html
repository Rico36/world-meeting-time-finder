The time difference

For most of the year, Auckland is 5 hours ahead of Jakarta. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 4 April: Auckland is 6 hours ahead of Jakarta
  • 5 April to 26 September: Auckland is 5 hours ahead of Jakarta
  • 27 September to 31 December: Auckland is 6 hours ahead of Jakarta

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 2 PM in Auckland lands at 9 AM in Jakarta — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Clock changes to plan around

  • Auckland changes clocks — 2026: 5 April and 27 September; 2027: 4 April and 26 September.
  • Jakarta does not change clocks.

Questions about Auckland and Jakarta

What time is it in Jakarta when it is 9 AM in Auckland?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Auckland is 4 AM in Jakarta (UTC+12 against UTC+7). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Auckland or Jakarta change clocks first?

Jakarta does not observe daylight saving time; Auckland does, so the gap shifts when Auckland changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Auckland or Jakarta soon?

Auckland: Labour Day on 26 October. Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December.
