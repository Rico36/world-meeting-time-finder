The time difference

For most of the year, Singapore is 6 hours ahead of Madrid. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Singapore is 7 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 29 March to 24 October: Singapore is 6 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 25 October to 31 December: Singapore is 7 hours ahead of Madrid

Questions about Madrid and Singapore

What time is it in Singapore when it is 9 AM in Madrid?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Madrid is 3 PM in Singapore (UTC+2 against UTC+8). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Madrid or Singapore change clocks first?

Singapore does not observe daylight saving time; Madrid does, so the gap shifts when Madrid changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Madrid or Singapore soon?

Madrid: National Day on 12 October. Singapore: Diwali on 8 November.
