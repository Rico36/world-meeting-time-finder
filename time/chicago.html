The Chicago time zone

Chicago moves between UTC-6 and UTC-5 across the year. Chicago changes clocks — 2026: 8 March and 1 November; 2027: 14 March and 7 November. Any city pairing below that involves a fixed-offset city will shift by an hour on those dates.
