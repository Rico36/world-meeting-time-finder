Questions about São Paulo and Seoul

When is the best time to meet between São Paulo and Seoul?

There is no hour that is inside normal working time in both cities, so someone has to stretch. The least painful slot is 6:30 AM in São Paulo / 6:30 PM in Seoul. At this distance the fair answer is usually to alternate which side takes the awkward hour rather than fixing one time forever.

What time is it in Seoul when it is 9 AM in São Paulo?

For most of the year, 9 AM in São Paulo is 9 PM in Seoul (UTC-3 against UTC+9). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does São Paulo or Seoul change clocks first?

Neither São Paulo nor Seoul observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in São Paulo or Seoul soon?

São Paulo: Our Lady of Aparecida on 12 October. Seoul: The day preceding Chuseok on 24 September.
