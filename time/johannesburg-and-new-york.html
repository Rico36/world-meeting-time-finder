The time difference

For most of the year, Johannesburg is 6 hours ahead of New York. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Johannesburg is 7 hours ahead of New York
  • 8 March to 31 October: Johannesburg is 6 hours ahead of New York
  • 1 November to 31 December: Johannesburg is 7 hours ahead of New York

Questions about Johannesburg and New York

What time is it in New York when it is 9 AM in Johannesburg?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Johannesburg is 3 AM in New York (UTC+2 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Johannesburg or New York change clocks first?

Johannesburg does not observe daylight saving time; New York does, so the gap shifts when New York changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Johannesburg or New York soon?

Johannesburg: Heritage Day on 24 September. New York: Columbus Day on 12 October.
