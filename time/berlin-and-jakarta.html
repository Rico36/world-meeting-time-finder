The time difference

For most of the year, Jakarta is 5 hours ahead of Berlin. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Jakarta is 6 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 29 March to 24 October: Jakarta is 5 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 25 October to 31 December: Jakarta is 6 hours ahead of Berlin

Questions about Berlin and Jakarta

When is the best time to meet between Berlin and Jakarta?

Aim for 9 AM in Berlin, which is 2 PM in Jakarta. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Jakarta when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 2 PM in Jakarta (UTC+2 against UTC+7). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or Jakarta change clocks first?

Jakarta does not observe daylight saving time; Berlin does, so the gap shifts when Berlin changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or Jakarta soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December.
