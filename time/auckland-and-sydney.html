The time difference

For most of the year, Auckland is 2 hours ahead of Sydney. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 26 September: Auckland is 2 hours ahead of Sydney
  • 27 September to 3 October: Auckland is 3 hours ahead of Sydney
  • 4 October to 31 December: Auckland is 2 hours ahead of Sydney

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 11 AM in Auckland lands at 9 AM in Sydney — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Auckland and Sydney

What time is it in Sydney when it is 9 AM in Auckland?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Auckland is 7 AM in Sydney (UTC+12 against UTC+10). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Auckland or Sydney change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Auckland or Sydney soon?

Auckland: Labour Day on 26 October. Sydney: Christmas Day on 25 December.
