Questions about Singapore and Tokyo

When is the best time to meet between Singapore and Tokyo?

Aim for 9 AM in Singapore, which is 10 AM in Tokyo. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Tokyo when it is 9 AM in Singapore?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Singapore is 10 AM in Tokyo (UTC+8 against UTC+9). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Singapore or Tokyo change clocks first?

Neither Singapore nor Tokyo observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Singapore or Tokyo soon?

Singapore: Diwali on 8 November. Tokyo: Respect for the Aged Day on 21 September.
