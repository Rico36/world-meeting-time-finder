The time difference

For most of the year, Sydney is 1 hours ahead of Seoul. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 4 April: Sydney is 2 hours ahead of Seoul
  • 5 April to 3 October: Sydney is 1 hours ahead of Seoul
  • 4 October to 31 December: Sydney is 2 hours ahead of Seoul

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in Seoul lands at 10 AM in Sydney — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Seoul and Sydney

What time is it in Sydney when it is 9 AM in Seoul?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Seoul is 10 AM in Sydney (UTC+9 against UTC+10). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Seoul or Sydney change clocks first?

Seoul does not observe daylight saving time; Sydney does, so the gap shifts when Sydney changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Seoul or Sydney soon?

Seoul: The day preceding Chuseok on 24 September. Sydney: Christmas Day on 25 December.
