The time difference

For most of the year, Amsterdam is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Amsterdam is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 8 March to 28 March: Amsterdam is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 29 March to 24 October: Amsterdam is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 25 October to 31 October: Amsterdam is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 1 November to 31 December: Amsterdam is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles

Best time to meet

Amsterdam and Los Angeles do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 5 PM in Amsterdam / 8 AM in Los Angeles — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Amsterdam and Los Angeles

What time is it in Los Angeles when it is 9 AM in Amsterdam?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Amsterdam is 12 AM in Los Angeles (UTC+2 against UTC-7). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Amsterdam or Los Angeles change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Amsterdam or Los Angeles soon?

Amsterdam: Christmas Day on 25 December. Los Angeles: Columbus Day on 12 October.
