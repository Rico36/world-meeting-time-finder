The time difference

For most of the year, Johannesburg is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Johannesburg is 10 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 8 March to 31 October: Johannesburg is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 1 November to 31 December: Johannesburg is 10 hours ahead of Los Angeles

Best time to meet

Johannesburg and Los Angeles do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 5 PM in Johannesburg / 8 AM in Los Angeles — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Johannesburg and Los Angeles

What time is it in Los Angeles when it is 9 AM in Johannesburg?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Johannesburg is 12 AM in Los Angeles (UTC+2 against UTC-7). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Johannesburg or Los Angeles change clocks first?

Johannesburg does not observe daylight saving time; Los Angeles does, so the gap shifts when Los Angeles changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Johannesburg or Los Angeles soon?

Johannesburg: Heritage Day on 24 September. Los Angeles: Columbus Day on 12 October.
