The time difference

For most of the year, Sydney is 6 hours ahead of Dubai. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 4 April: Sydney is 7 hours ahead of Dubai
  • 5 April to 3 October: Sydney is 6 hours ahead of Dubai
  • 4 October to 31 December: Sydney is 7 hours ahead of Dubai

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in Dubai lands at 3 PM in Sydney — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Clock changes to plan around

  • Dubai does not change clocks.
  • Sydney changes clocks — 2026: 5 April and 4 October; 2027: 4 April and 3 October.

Questions about Dubai and Sydney

What time is it in Sydney when it is 9 AM in Dubai?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Dubai is 3 PM in Sydney (UTC+4 against UTC+10). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Dubai or Sydney change clocks first?

Dubai does not observe daylight saving time; Sydney does, so the gap shifts when Sydney changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Dubai or Sydney soon?

Dubai: National Day on 2 December. Sydney: Christmas Day on 25 December.
