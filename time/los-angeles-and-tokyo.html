The time difference

For most of the year, Tokyo is 16 hours ahead of Los Angeles. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Tokyo is 17 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 8 March to 31 October: Tokyo is 16 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 1 November to 31 December: Tokyo is 17 hours ahead of Los Angeles

Best time to meet

Los Angeles and Tokyo do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 4:30 PM in Los Angeles / 8:30 AM in Tokyo — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Los Angeles and Tokyo

What time is it in Tokyo when it is 9 AM in Los Angeles?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Los Angeles is 1 AM in Tokyo (UTC-7 against UTC+9). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Los Angeles or Tokyo change clocks first?

Tokyo does not observe daylight saving time; Los Angeles does, so the gap shifts when Los Angeles changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Los Angeles or Tokyo soon?

Los Angeles: Columbus Day on 12 October. Tokyo: Respect for the Aged Day on 21 September.
