The time difference

For most of the year, Madrid is 5 hours ahead of São Paulo. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Madrid is 4 hours ahead of São Paulo
  • 29 March to 24 October: Madrid is 5 hours ahead of São Paulo
  • 25 October to 31 December: Madrid is 4 hours ahead of São Paulo

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 2 PM in Madrid lands at 9 AM in São Paulo — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Clock changes to plan around

  • Madrid changes clocks — 2026: 29 March and 25 October; 2027: 28 March and 31 October.
  • São Paulo does not change clocks.

Questions about Madrid and São Paulo

What time is it in São Paulo when it is 9 AM in Madrid?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Madrid is 4 AM in São Paulo (UTC+2 against UTC-3). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Madrid or São Paulo change clocks first?

São Paulo does not observe daylight saving time; Madrid does, so the gap shifts when Madrid changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Madrid or São Paulo soon?

Madrid: National Day on 12 October. São Paulo: Our Lady of Aparecida on 12 October.
