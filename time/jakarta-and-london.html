The time difference

For most of the year, Jakarta is 6 hours ahead of London. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Jakarta is 7 hours ahead of London
  • 29 March to 24 October: Jakarta is 6 hours ahead of London
  • 25 October to 31 December: Jakarta is 7 hours ahead of London

A one-hour meeting starting around 3 PM in Jakarta lands at 9 AM in London — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Jakarta and London

What time is it in London when it is 9 AM in Jakarta?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Jakarta is 3 AM in London (UTC+7 against UTC+1). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Jakarta or London change clocks first?

Jakarta does not observe daylight saving time; London does, so the gap shifts when London changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Jakarta or London soon?

Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December. London: Christmas Day on 25 December.
