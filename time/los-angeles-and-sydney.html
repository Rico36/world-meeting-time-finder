The time difference

For most of the year, Sydney is 17 hours ahead of Los Angeles. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Sydney is 19 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 8 March to 4 April: Sydney is 18 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 5 April to 3 October: Sydney is 17 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 4 October to 31 October: Sydney is 18 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 1 November to 31 December: Sydney is 19 hours ahead of Los Angeles

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 4 PM in Los Angeles lands at 9 AM in Sydney — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Los Angeles and Sydney

What time is it in Sydney when it is 9 AM in Los Angeles?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Los Angeles is 2 AM in Sydney (UTC-7 against UTC+10). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Los Angeles or Sydney change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Los Angeles or Sydney soon?

Los Angeles: Columbus Day on 12 October. Sydney: Christmas Day on 25 December.
