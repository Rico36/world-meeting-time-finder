Questions about Mexico City and Singapore

When is the best time to meet between Mexico City and Singapore?

There is no hour that is inside normal working time in both cities, so someone has to stretch. The least painful slot is 5:30 PM in Mexico City / 7:30 AM in Singapore. At this distance the fair answer is usually to alternate which side takes the awkward hour rather than fixing one time forever.

What time is it in Singapore when it is 9 AM in Mexico City?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Mexico City is 11 PM in Singapore (UTC-6 against UTC+8). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Mexico City or Singapore change clocks first?

Neither Mexico City nor Singapore observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Mexico City or Singapore soon?

Mexico City: Independence Day on 16 September. Singapore: Diwali on 8 November.
