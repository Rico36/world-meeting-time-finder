The time difference

For most of the year, Berlin is 8 hours ahead of Mexico City. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Berlin is 7 hours ahead of Mexico City
  • 29 March to 24 October: Berlin is 8 hours ahead of Mexico City
  • 25 October to 31 December: Berlin is 7 hours ahead of Mexico City

Best time to meet

Berlin and Mexico City do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 4:30 PM in Berlin / 8:30 AM in Mexico City — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Berlin and Mexico City

When is the best time to meet between Berlin and Mexico City?

There is no hour that is inside normal working time in both cities, so someone has to stretch. The least painful slot is 4:30 PM in Berlin / 8:30 AM in Mexico City. At this distance the fair answer is usually to alternate which side takes the awkward hour rather than fixing one time forever.

What time is it in Mexico City when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 1 AM in Mexico City (UTC+2 against UTC-6). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or Mexico City change clocks first?

Mexico City does not observe daylight saving time; Berlin does, so the gap shifts when Berlin changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or Mexico City soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. Mexico City: Independence Day on 16 September.
