Questions about London and Paris

What time is it in Paris when it is 9 AM in London?

For most of the year, 9 AM in London is 10 AM in Paris (UTC+1 against UTC+2). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does London or Paris change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in London or Paris soon?

London: Christmas Day on 25 December. Paris: All Saints' Day on 1 November.
