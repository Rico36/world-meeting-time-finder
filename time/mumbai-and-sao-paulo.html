Questions about Mumbai and São Paulo

What time is it in São Paulo when it is 9 AM in Mumbai?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Mumbai is 12:30 AM in São Paulo (UTC+5:30 against UTC-3). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Mumbai or São Paulo change clocks first?

Neither Mumbai nor São Paulo observes daylight saving time.
