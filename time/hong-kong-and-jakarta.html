Questions about Hong Kong and Jakarta

What time is it in Jakarta when it is 9 AM in Hong Kong?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Hong Kong is 8 AM in Jakarta (UTC+8 against UTC+7). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Hong Kong or Jakarta change clocks first?

Neither Hong Kong nor Jakarta observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Hong Kong or Jakarta soon?

Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September. Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December.
