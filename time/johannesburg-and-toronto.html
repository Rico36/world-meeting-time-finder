Questions about Johannesburg and Toronto

When is the best time to meet between Johannesburg and Toronto?

Aim for 3 PM in Johannesburg, which is 9 AM in Toronto. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Toronto when it is 9 AM in Johannesburg?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Johannesburg is 3 AM in Toronto (UTC+2 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Johannesburg or Toronto change clocks first?

Johannesburg does not observe daylight saving time; Toronto does, so the gap shifts when Toronto changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Johannesburg or Toronto soon?

Johannesburg: Heritage Day on 24 September. Toronto: Christmas Day on 25 December.
