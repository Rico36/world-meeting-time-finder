The time difference

For most of the year, Jakarta is 5 hours ahead of Madrid. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Jakarta is 6 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 29 March to 24 October: Jakarta is 5 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 25 October to 31 December: Jakarta is 6 hours ahead of Madrid

Questions about Jakarta and Madrid

When is the best time to meet between Jakarta and Madrid?

Aim for 2 PM in Jakarta, which is 9 AM in Madrid. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Madrid when it is 9 AM in Jakarta?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Jakarta is 4 AM in Madrid (UTC+7 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Jakarta or Madrid change clocks first?

Jakarta does not observe daylight saving time; Madrid does, so the gap shifts when Madrid changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Jakarta or Madrid soon?

Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December. Madrid: National Day on 12 October.
