The time difference

For most of the year, Mumbai is 3.5 hours ahead of Paris. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Mumbai is 4.5 hours ahead of Paris
  • 29 March to 24 October: Mumbai is 3.5 hours ahead of Paris
  • 25 October to 31 December: Mumbai is 4.5 hours ahead of Paris

Questions about Mumbai and Paris

What time is it in Paris when it is 9 AM in Mumbai?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Mumbai is 5:30 AM in Paris (UTC+5:30 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Mumbai or Paris change clocks first?

Mumbai does not observe daylight saving time; Paris does, so the gap shifts when Paris changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Mumbai or Paris soon?

Mumbai: Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on 2 October. Paris: All Saints' Day on 1 November.
