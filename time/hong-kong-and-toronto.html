The time difference

For most of the year, Hong Kong is 12 hours ahead of Toronto. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Hong Kong is 13 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 8 March to 31 October: Hong Kong is 12 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 1 November to 31 December: Hong Kong is 13 hours ahead of Toronto

Best time to meet

Hong Kong and Toronto do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 6:30 AM in Hong Kong / 6:30 PM in Toronto — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Hong Kong and Toronto

What time is it in Toronto when it is 9 AM in Hong Kong?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Hong Kong is 9 PM in Toronto (UTC+8 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Hong Kong or Toronto change clocks first?

Hong Kong does not observe daylight saving time; Toronto does, so the gap shifts when Toronto changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Hong Kong or Toronto soon?

Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September. Toronto: Christmas Day on 25 December.
