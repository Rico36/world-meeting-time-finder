The time difference

For most of the year, Paris is 5 hours ahead of São Paulo. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Paris is 4 hours ahead of São Paulo
  • 29 March to 24 October: Paris is 5 hours ahead of São Paulo
  • 25 October to 31 December: Paris is 4 hours ahead of São Paulo

Questions about Paris and São Paulo

What time is it in São Paulo when it is 9 AM in Paris?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Paris is 4 AM in São Paulo (UTC+2 against UTC-3). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Paris or São Paulo change clocks first?

São Paulo does not observe daylight saving time; Paris does, so the gap shifts when Paris changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Paris or São Paulo soon?

Paris: All Saints' Day on 1 November. São Paulo: Our Lady of Aparecida on 12 October.
