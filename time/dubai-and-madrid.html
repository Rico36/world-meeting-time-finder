The time difference

For most of the year, Dubai is 2 hours ahead of Madrid. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Dubai is 3 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 29 March to 24 October: Dubai is 2 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 25 October to 31 December: Dubai is 3 hours ahead of Madrid

Questions about Dubai and Madrid

When is the best time to meet between Dubai and Madrid?

Aim for 11 AM in Dubai, which is 9 AM in Madrid. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Madrid when it is 9 AM in Dubai?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Dubai is 7 AM in Madrid (UTC+4 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Dubai or Madrid change clocks first?

Dubai does not observe daylight saving time; Madrid does, so the gap shifts when Madrid changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Dubai or Madrid soon?

Dubai: National Day on 2 December. Madrid: National Day on 12 October.
