Questions about São Paulo and Singapore

What time is it in Singapore when it is 9 AM in São Paulo?

For most of the year, 9 AM in São Paulo is 8 PM in Singapore (UTC-3 against UTC+8). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does São Paulo or Singapore change clocks first?

Neither São Paulo nor Singapore observes daylight saving time.
