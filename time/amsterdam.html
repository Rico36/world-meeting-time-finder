The Amsterdam time zone

Amsterdam moves between UTC+1 and UTC+2 across the year. Amsterdam changes clocks — 2026: 29 March and 25 October; 2027: 28 March and 31 October. Any city pairing below that involves a fixed-offset city will shift by an hour on those dates.
