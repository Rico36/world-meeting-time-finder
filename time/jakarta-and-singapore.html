Questions about Jakarta and Singapore

What time is it in Singapore when it is 9 AM in Jakarta?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Jakarta is 10 AM in Singapore (UTC+7 against UTC+8). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Jakarta or Singapore change clocks first?

Neither Jakarta nor Singapore observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Jakarta or Singapore soon?

Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December. Singapore: Diwali on 8 November.
