Questions about Hong Kong and Johannesburg

What time is it in Johannesburg when it is 9 AM in Hong Kong?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Hong Kong is 3 AM in Johannesburg (UTC+8 against UTC+2). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Hong Kong or Johannesburg change clocks first?

Neither Hong Kong nor Johannesburg observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Hong Kong or Johannesburg soon?

Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September. Johannesburg: Heritage Day on 24 September.
