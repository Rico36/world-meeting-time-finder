The time difference

For most of the year, London is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: London is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 8 March to 28 March: London is 7 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 29 March to 24 October: London is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 25 October to 31 October: London is 7 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 1 November to 31 December: London is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles

Best time to meet

London and Los Angeles do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 4:30 PM in London / 8:30 AM in Los Angeles — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about London and Los Angeles

What time is it in Los Angeles when it is 9 AM in London?

For most of the year, 9 AM in London is 1 AM in Los Angeles (UTC+1 against UTC-7). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does London or Los Angeles change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in London or Los Angeles soon?

London: Christmas Day on 25 December. Los Angeles: Columbus Day on 12 October.
