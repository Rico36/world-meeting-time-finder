Questions about Chicago and New York

When is the best time to meet between Chicago and New York?

Aim for 9 AM in Chicago, which is 10 AM in New York. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in New York when it is 9 AM in Chicago?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Chicago is 10 AM in New York (UTC-5 against UTC-4). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Chicago or New York change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Chicago: Columbus Day on 12 October. New York: Columbus Day on 12 October.
