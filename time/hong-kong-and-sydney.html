The time difference

For most of the year, Sydney is 2 hours ahead of Hong Kong. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 4 April: Sydney is 3 hours ahead of Hong Kong
  • 5 April to 3 October: Sydney is 2 hours ahead of Hong Kong
  • 4 October to 31 December: Sydney is 3 hours ahead of Hong Kong

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in Hong Kong lands at 11 AM in Sydney — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Clock changes to plan around

  • Hong Kong does not change clocks.
  • Sydney changes clocks — 2026: 5 April and 4 October; 2027: 4 April and 3 October.

Questions about Hong Kong and Sydney

What time is it in Sydney when it is 9 AM in Hong Kong?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Hong Kong is 11 AM in Sydney (UTC+8 against UTC+10). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Hong Kong or Sydney change clocks first?

Hong Kong does not observe daylight saving time; Sydney does, so the gap shifts when Sydney changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Hong Kong or Sydney soon?

Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September. Sydney: Christmas Day on 25 December.
