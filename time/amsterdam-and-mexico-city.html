The time difference

For most of the year, Amsterdam is 8 hours ahead of Mexico City. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Amsterdam is 7 hours ahead of Mexico City
  • 29 March to 24 October: Amsterdam is 8 hours ahead of Mexico City
  • 25 October to 31 December: Amsterdam is 7 hours ahead of Mexico City

Best time to meet

Amsterdam and Mexico City do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 4:30 PM in Amsterdam / 8:30 AM in Mexico City — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Clock changes to plan around

  • Amsterdam changes clocks — 2026: 29 March and 25 October; 2027: 28 March and 31 October.
  • Mexico City does not change clocks.

Questions about Amsterdam and Mexico City

What time is it in Mexico City when it is 9 AM in Amsterdam?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Amsterdam is 1 AM in Mexico City (UTC+2 against UTC-6). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Amsterdam or Mexico City change clocks first?

Mexico City does not observe daylight saving time; Amsterdam does, so the gap shifts when Amsterdam changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Amsterdam or Mexico City soon?

Amsterdam: Christmas Day on 25 December. Mexico City: Independence Day on 16 September.
