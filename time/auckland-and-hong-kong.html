The time difference

For most of the year, Auckland is 4 hours ahead of Hong Kong. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 4 April: Auckland is 5 hours ahead of Hong Kong
  • 5 April to 26 September: Auckland is 4 hours ahead of Hong Kong
  • 27 September to 31 December: Auckland is 5 hours ahead of Hong Kong

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 1 PM in Auckland lands at 9 AM in Hong Kong — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Auckland and Hong Kong

When is the best time to meet between Auckland and Hong Kong?

Aim for 1 PM in Auckland, which is 9 AM in Hong Kong. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Hong Kong when it is 9 AM in Auckland?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Auckland is 5 AM in Hong Kong (UTC+12 against UTC+8). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Auckland or Hong Kong change clocks first?

Hong Kong does not observe daylight saving time; Auckland does, so the gap shifts when Auckland changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Auckland or Hong Kong soon?

Auckland: Labour Day on 26 October. Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September.
