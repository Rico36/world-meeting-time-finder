The time difference

For most of the year, Seoul is 14 hours ahead of Chicago. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Seoul is 15 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 8 March to 31 October: Seoul is 14 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 1 November to 31 December: Seoul is 15 hours ahead of Chicago

Best time to meet

Chicago and Seoul do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 5:30 PM in Chicago / 7:30 AM in Seoul — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Chicago and Seoul

What time is it in Seoul when it is 9 AM in Chicago?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Chicago is 11 PM in Seoul (UTC-5 against UTC+9). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Chicago or Seoul change clocks first?

Seoul does not observe daylight saving time; Chicago does, so the gap shifts when Chicago changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Chicago or Seoul soon?

Chicago: Columbus Day on 12 October. Seoul: The day preceding Chuseok on 24 September.
