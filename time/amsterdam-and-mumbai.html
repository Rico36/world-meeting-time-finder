The time difference

For most of the year, Mumbai is 3.5 hours ahead of Amsterdam. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Mumbai is 4.5 hours ahead of Amsterdam
  • 29 March to 24 October: Mumbai is 3.5 hours ahead of Amsterdam
  • 25 October to 31 December: Mumbai is 4.5 hours ahead of Amsterdam

Questions about Amsterdam and Mumbai

When is the best time to meet between Amsterdam and Mumbai?

Aim for 9 AM in Amsterdam, which is 12:30 PM in Mumbai. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Mumbai when it is 9 AM in Amsterdam?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Amsterdam is 12:30 PM in Mumbai (UTC+2 against UTC+5:30). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Amsterdam or Mumbai change clocks first?

Mumbai does not observe daylight saving time; Amsterdam does, so the gap shifts when Amsterdam changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Amsterdam or Mumbai soon?

Amsterdam: Christmas Day on 25 December. Mumbai: Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on 2 October.
