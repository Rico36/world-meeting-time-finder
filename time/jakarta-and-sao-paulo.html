Questions about Jakarta and São Paulo

What time is it in São Paulo when it is 9 AM in Jakarta?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Jakarta is 11 PM in São Paulo (UTC+7 against UTC-3). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Jakarta or São Paulo change clocks first?

Neither Jakarta nor São Paulo observes daylight saving time.

Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December. São Paulo: Our Lady of Aparecida on 12 October.
