Questions about London and Madrid

When is the best time to meet between London and Madrid?

Aim for 9 AM in London, which is 10 AM in Madrid. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Madrid when it is 9 AM in London?

For most of the year, 9 AM in London is 10 AM in Madrid (UTC+1 against UTC+2). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does London or Madrid change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in London or Madrid soon?

London: Christmas Day on 25 December. Madrid: National Day on 12 October.
