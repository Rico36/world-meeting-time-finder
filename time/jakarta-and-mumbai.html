Questions about Jakarta and Mumbai

What time is it in Mumbai when it is 9 AM in Jakarta?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Jakarta is 7:30 AM in Mumbai (UTC+7 against UTC+5:30). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Jakarta or Mumbai change clocks first?

Neither Jakarta nor Mumbai observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Jakarta or Mumbai soon?

Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December. Mumbai: Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on 2 October.
