The Auckland time zone

Auckland moves between UTC+12 and UTC+13 across the year. Auckland changes clocks — 2026: 5 April and 27 September; 2027: 4 April and 26 September. Any city pairing below that involves a fixed-offset city will shift by an hour on those dates.
