The time difference

For most of the year, Jakarta is 12 hours ahead of Chicago. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Jakarta is 13 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 8 March to 31 October: Jakarta is 12 hours ahead of Chicago
  • 1 November to 31 December: Jakarta is 13 hours ahead of Chicago

Best time to meet

Chicago and Jakarta do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 6:30 AM in Chicago / 6:30 PM in Jakarta — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Clock changes to plan around

  • Chicago changes clocks — 2026: 8 March and 1 November; 2027: 14 March and 7 November.
  • Jakarta does not change clocks.

Questions about Chicago and Jakarta

What time is it in Jakarta when it is 9 AM in Chicago?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Chicago is 9 PM in Jakarta (UTC-5 against UTC+7). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Chicago or Jakarta change clocks first?

Jakarta does not observe daylight saving time; Chicago does, so the gap shifts when Chicago changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Chicago or Jakarta soon?

Chicago: Columbus Day on 12 October. Jakarta: Christmas Day on 25 December.
