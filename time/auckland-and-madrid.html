The time difference

For most of the year, Auckland is 10 hours ahead of Madrid. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Auckland is 12 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 29 March to 4 April: Auckland is 11 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 5 April to 26 September: Auckland is 10 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 27 September to 24 October: Auckland is 11 hours ahead of Madrid
  • 25 October to 31 December: Auckland is 12 hours ahead of Madrid

Best time to meet

Auckland and Madrid do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 5:30 PM in Auckland / 7:30 AM in Madrid — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Auckland and Madrid

What time is it in Madrid when it is 9 AM in Auckland?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Auckland is 11 PM in Madrid (UTC+12 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Auckland or Madrid change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Auckland or Madrid soon?

Auckland: Labour Day on 26 October. Madrid: National Day on 12 October.
