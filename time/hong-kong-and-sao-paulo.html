Questions about Hong Kong and São Paulo

When is the best time to meet between Hong Kong and São Paulo?

There is no hour that is inside normal working time in both cities, so someone has to stretch. The least painful slot is 6 PM in Hong Kong / 7 AM in São Paulo. At this distance the fair answer is usually to alternate which side takes the awkward hour rather than fixing one time forever.

What time is it in São Paulo when it is 9 AM in Hong Kong?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Hong Kong is 10 PM in São Paulo (UTC+8 against UTC-3). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Hong Kong or São Paulo change clocks first?

Neither Hong Kong nor São Paulo observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Hong Kong or São Paulo soon?

Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September. São Paulo: Our Lady of Aparecida on 12 October.
