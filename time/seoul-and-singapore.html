Questions about Seoul and Singapore

What time is it in Singapore when it is 9 AM in Seoul?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Seoul is 8 AM in Singapore (UTC+9 against UTC+8). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Seoul or Singapore change clocks first?

Neither Seoul nor Singapore observes daylight saving time.

Seoul: The day preceding Chuseok on 24 September. Singapore: Diwali on 8 November.
