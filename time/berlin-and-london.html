Questions about Berlin and London

What time is it in London when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 8 AM in London (UTC+2 against UTC+1). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or London change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or London soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. London: Christmas Day on 25 December.
