The time difference

For most of the year, Dubai is 2 hours ahead of Amsterdam. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Dubai is 3 hours ahead of Amsterdam
  • 29 March to 24 October: Dubai is 2 hours ahead of Amsterdam
  • 25 October to 31 December: Dubai is 3 hours ahead of Amsterdam

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in Amsterdam lands at 11 AM in Dubai — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Amsterdam and Dubai

What time is it in Dubai when it is 9 AM in Amsterdam?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Amsterdam is 11 AM in Dubai (UTC+2 against UTC+4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Amsterdam or Dubai change clocks first?

Dubai does not observe daylight saving time; Amsterdam does, so the gap shifts when Amsterdam changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Amsterdam or Dubai soon?

Amsterdam: Christmas Day on 25 December. Dubai: National Day on 2 December.
