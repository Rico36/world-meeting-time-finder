Questions about São Paulo and Toronto

When is the best time to meet between São Paulo and Toronto?

Aim for 10 AM in São Paulo, which is 9 AM in Toronto. That keeps both sides inside a normal 9 AM–5 PM day, so neither is dialling in before breakfast or after dinner. Check the date against each country's public holidays before sending the invite.

What time is it in Toronto when it is 9 AM in São Paulo?

For most of the year, 9 AM in São Paulo is 8 AM in Toronto (UTC-3 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does São Paulo or Toronto change clocks first?

São Paulo does not observe daylight saving time; Toronto does, so the gap shifts when Toronto changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in São Paulo or Toronto soon?

São Paulo: Our Lady of Aparecida on 12 October. Toronto: Christmas Day on 25 December.
