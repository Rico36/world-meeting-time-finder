The time difference

For most of the year, Dubai is 8 hours ahead of Toronto. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Dubai is 9 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 8 March to 31 October: Dubai is 8 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 1 November to 31 December: Dubai is 9 hours ahead of Toronto

Best time to meet

Dubai and Toronto do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 4:30 PM in Dubai / 8:30 AM in Toronto — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Dubai and Toronto

What time is it in Toronto when it is 9 AM in Dubai?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Dubai is 1 AM in Toronto (UTC+4 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Dubai or Toronto change clocks first?

Dubai does not observe daylight saving time; Toronto does, so the gap shifts when Toronto changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Dubai or Toronto soon?

Dubai: National Day on 2 December. Toronto: Christmas Day on 25 December.
