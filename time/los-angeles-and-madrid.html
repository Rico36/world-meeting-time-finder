The time difference

For most of the year, Madrid is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Madrid is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 8 March to 28 March: Madrid is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 29 March to 24 October: Madrid is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 25 October to 31 October: Madrid is 8 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • 1 November to 31 December: Madrid is 9 hours ahead of Los Angeles

Best time to meet

Los Angeles and Madrid do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 8 AM in Los Angeles / 5 PM in Madrid — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

  • Madrid changes clocks — 2026: 29 March and 25 October; 2027: 28 March and 31 October.

Questions about Los Angeles and Madrid

What time is it in Madrid when it is 9 AM in Los Angeles?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Los Angeles is 6 PM in Madrid (UTC-7 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Los Angeles or Madrid change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Los Angeles or Madrid soon?

Los Angeles: Columbus Day on 12 October. Madrid: National Day on 12 October.
