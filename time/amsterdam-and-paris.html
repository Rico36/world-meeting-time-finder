Questions about Amsterdam and Paris

What time is it in Paris when it is 9 AM in Amsterdam?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Amsterdam is 9 AM in Paris (UTC+2 against UTC+2). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Amsterdam or Paris change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.
