Questions about Berlin and Paris

What time is it in Paris when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 9 AM in Paris (UTC+2 against UTC+2). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or Paris change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. Paris: All Saints' Day on 1 November.
