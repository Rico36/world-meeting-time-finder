The time difference

For most of the year, São Paulo is 1 hours ahead of New York. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: São Paulo is 2 hours ahead of New York
  • 8 March to 31 October: São Paulo is 1 hours ahead of New York
  • 1 November to 31 December: São Paulo is 2 hours ahead of New York

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in New York lands at 10 AM in São Paulo — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Clock changes to plan around

  • New York changes clocks — 2026: 8 March and 1 November; 2027: 14 March and 7 November.
  • São Paulo does not change clocks.

Questions about New York and São Paulo

What time is it in São Paulo when it is 9 AM in New York?

For most of the year, 9 AM in New York is 10 AM in São Paulo (UTC-4 against UTC-3). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does New York or São Paulo change clocks first?

São Paulo does not observe daylight saving time; New York does, so the gap shifts when New York changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in New York or São Paulo soon?

New York: Columbus Day on 12 October. São Paulo: Our Lady of Aparecida on 12 October.
