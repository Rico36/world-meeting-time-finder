The time difference

For most of the year, Dubai is 2 hours ahead of Paris. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Dubai is 3 hours ahead of Paris
  • 29 March to 24 October: Dubai is 2 hours ahead of Paris
  • 25 October to 31 December: Dubai is 3 hours ahead of Paris

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 11 AM in Dubai lands at 9 AM in Paris — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Clock changes to plan around

  • Paris changes clocks — 2026: 29 March and 25 October; 2027: 28 March and 31 October.

Questions about Dubai and Paris

What time is it in Paris when it is 9 AM in Dubai?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Dubai is 7 AM in Paris (UTC+4 against UTC+2). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Dubai or Paris change clocks first?

Dubai does not observe daylight saving time; Paris does, so the gap shifts when Paris changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Dubai or Paris soon?

Dubai: National Day on 2 December. Paris: All Saints' Day on 1 November.
