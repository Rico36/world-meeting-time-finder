The time difference

For most of the year, Auckland is 3 hours ahead of Seoul. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 4 April: Auckland is 4 hours ahead of Seoul
  • 5 April to 26 September: Auckland is 3 hours ahead of Seoul
  • 27 September to 31 December: Auckland is 4 hours ahead of Seoul

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 12 PM in Auckland lands at 9 AM in Seoul — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Auckland and Seoul

What time is it in Seoul when it is 9 AM in Auckland?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Auckland is 6 AM in Seoul (UTC+12 against UTC+9). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Auckland or Seoul change clocks first?

Seoul does not observe daylight saving time; Auckland does, so the gap shifts when Auckland changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Auckland or Seoul soon?

Auckland: Labour Day on 26 October. Seoul: The day preceding Chuseok on 24 September.
