The time difference

For most of the year, Madrid is 6 hours ahead of Toronto. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Madrid is 6 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 8 March to 28 March: Madrid is 5 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 29 March to 24 October: Madrid is 6 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 25 October to 31 October: Madrid is 5 hours ahead of Toronto
  • 1 November to 31 December: Madrid is 6 hours ahead of Toronto

Questions about Madrid and Toronto

What time is it in Toronto when it is 9 AM in Madrid?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Madrid is 3 AM in Toronto (UTC+2 against UTC-4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Madrid or Toronto change clocks first?

Both cities observe daylight saving, but not always on the same dates — see the clock-change dates above.

Is there a public holiday in Madrid or Toronto soon?

Madrid: National Day on 12 October. Toronto: Christmas Day on 25 December.
