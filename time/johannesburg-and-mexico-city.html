Questions about Johannesburg and Mexico City

When is the best time to meet between Johannesburg and Mexico City?

There is no hour that is inside normal working time in both cities, so someone has to stretch. The least painful slot is 4:30 PM in Johannesburg / 8:30 AM in Mexico City. At this distance the fair answer is usually to alternate which side takes the awkward hour rather than fixing one time forever.

What time is it in Mexico City when it is 9 AM in Johannesburg?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Johannesburg is 1 AM in Mexico City (UTC+2 against UTC-6). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Johannesburg or Mexico City change clocks first?

Neither Johannesburg nor Mexico City observes daylight saving time.

Is there a public holiday in Johannesburg or Mexico City soon?

Johannesburg: Heritage Day on 24 September. Mexico City: Independence Day on 16 September.
