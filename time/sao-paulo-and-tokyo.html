Questions about São Paulo and Tokyo

What time is it in Tokyo when it is 9 AM in São Paulo?

For most of the year, 9 AM in São Paulo is 9 PM in Tokyo (UTC-3 against UTC+9). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does São Paulo or Tokyo change clocks first?

Neither São Paulo nor Tokyo observes daylight saving time.
