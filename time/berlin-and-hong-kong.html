The time difference

For most of the year, Hong Kong is 6 hours ahead of Berlin. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 28 March: Hong Kong is 7 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 29 March to 24 October: Hong Kong is 6 hours ahead of Berlin
  • 25 October to 31 December: Hong Kong is 7 hours ahead of Berlin

Best time to meet

A one-hour meeting starting around 9 AM in Berlin lands at 3 PM in Hong Kong — inside normal working hours (9 AM–5 PM) for both sides.

Questions about Berlin and Hong Kong

What time is it in Hong Kong when it is 9 AM in Berlin?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Berlin is 3 PM in Hong Kong (UTC+2 against UTC+8). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Berlin or Hong Kong change clocks first?

Hong Kong does not observe daylight saving time; Berlin does, so the gap shifts when Berlin changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Berlin or Hong Kong soon?

Berlin: German Unity Day on 3 October. Hong Kong: The Day following Mid-Autumn Festival on 26 September.
