The time difference

For most of the year, Tokyo is 13 hours ahead of New York. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 7 March: Tokyo is 14 hours ahead of New York
  • 8 March to 31 October: Tokyo is 13 hours ahead of New York
  • 1 November to 31 December: Tokyo is 14 hours ahead of New York

Best time to meet

New York and Tokyo do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 6 PM in New York / 7 AM in Tokyo — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about New York and Tokyo

What time is it in Tokyo when it is 9 AM in New York?

For most of the year, 9 AM in New York is 10 PM in Tokyo (UTC-4 against UTC+9). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does New York or Tokyo change clocks first?

Tokyo does not observe daylight saving time; New York does, so the gap shifts when New York changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in New York or Tokyo soon?

New York: Columbus Day on 12 October. Tokyo: Respect for the Aged Day on 21 September.
