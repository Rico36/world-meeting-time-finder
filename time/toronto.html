The Toronto time zone

Toronto moves between UTC-5 and UTC-4 across the year. Toronto changes clocks — 2026: 8 March and 1 November; 2027: 14 March and 7 November. Any city pairing below that involves a fixed-offset city will shift by an hour on those dates.
