Questions about Dubai and Mexico City

For most of the year, 9 AM in Dubai is 11 PM in Mexico City (UTC+4 against UTC-6). Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Dubai or Mexico City change clocks first?

Neither Dubai nor Mexico City observes daylight saving time.
