The time difference

For most of the year, Auckland is 8 hours ahead of Dubai. That gap does not hold all year — here is how it actually moves:

  • 1 January to 4 April: Auckland is 9 hours ahead of Dubai
  • 5 April to 26 September: Auckland is 8 hours ahead of Dubai
  • 27 September to 31 December: Auckland is 9 hours ahead of Dubai

Best time to meet

Auckland and Dubai do not share a normal working-hours window. The best compromise is 4:30 PM in Auckland / 8:30 AM in Dubai — still early or late for whoever is furthest from a normal day, which is the honest trade-off at this distance.

Questions about Auckland and Dubai

What time is it in Dubai when it is 9 AM in Auckland?

For most of the year, 9 AM in Auckland is 1 AM in Dubai (UTC+12 against UTC+4). During the weeks listed above when the gap shifts, it moves by an hour. Open the planner for a specific date.

Does Auckland or Dubai change clocks first?

Dubai does not observe daylight saving time; Auckland does, so the gap shifts when Auckland changes clocks.

Is there a public holiday in Auckland or Dubai soon?

Auckland: Labour Day on 26 October. Dubai: National Day on 2 December.
